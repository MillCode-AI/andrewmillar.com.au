Sound familiar?

The business is busy. The work is harder than it should be.

Most growing businesses reach a point where effort keeps rising but capacity doesn’t. The signs tend to look like this.

  • Capable people buried in administration

    Skilled staff spend a large part of their week on tasks that add little value, leaving less time for the work that does.

  • The same work, done twice

    Information gets re-entered and re-checked because processes don’t connect, and rework has quietly become normal.

  • Information that is hard to find or trust

    Documents live in inboxes and personal folders, so people ask around instead of looking it up.

  • Too much depends on a few people

    Work is done differently depending on who does it, and critical knowledge sits in one or two people’s heads.

  • Paying for Microsoft 365, using a fraction of it

    The tools are in place, but the way people work hasn’t really changed since they arrived.

  • Unsure where AI genuinely fits

    There’s pressure to do something with Copilot and AI, but little clarity about where it would help or what it takes to use it well.

These rarely start as technology problems. They usually come down to how the work is designed and supported.

How AMC works

Start with the work, not the technology.

“Why are we doing it?” comes before “How do we make it faster?”

Every engagement begins with the business outcome and how the work is really performed today. Only then does it make sense to decide what should change, and whether technology has a part to play.

Understand. Empower. Embed.

Understand

Create clarity about the outcome you need and how the work is really done today, including where friction and risk sit. Test the assumptions, then agree what is genuinely worth changing.

Empower

Build the capability and confidence people need to work differently, along with the conditions that make it possible. Enablement and training happen here, as part of the change rather than a separate exercise.

Embed

Make the improvement last. Clear ownership and sensible governance keep it in place, and a practical roadmap sets out what comes next.

AI adoption

The AI Adoption Framework - OURS

The OURS AI Adoption Framework describes how AI may improve, change or extend work.

  • Optimise

    Make existing work better.

    Use existing capabilities more effectively to remove friction, improve quality and reduce unnecessary effort.

  • Upgrade

    Improve how necessary work gets done.

    Use AI and automation to reduce manual effort, improve consistency and support faster outcomes and decisions.

  • Re-think

    Question how the work should be done.

    Challenge existing assumptions and consider whether the same outcome could be achieved in a fundamentally better way.

  • Scale

    Extend what has proven valuable.

    Apply successful approaches more broadly using the capability, confidence, governance and foundations already established.

Understanding the work comes first

OURS is not a sequence or a maturity ladder. The right opportunity depends on the work and the outcome you need, and different parts of the business may call for different responses.

Optimise is often the simplest place to start. People build confidence with AI in familiar work, using capability the organisation may already have. But there’s no value in optimising work that shouldn’t exist, or upgrading a process that needs re-thinking. And nothing should be scaled until there’s credible evidence it creates value and can be sustained.

Ways to start

Start small. Learn something real.

AMC prefers the smallest meaningful piece of work that creates clarity or worthwhile value. Engagements are focused and usually fixed fee, with a clear scope and a clear end point.

  • Work and process assessment

    A close look at how a specific area of work is performed, where effort is being lost and what is worth changing first.

  • Microsoft 365 and Copilot value review

    An independent view of how well your current tools are being used, and where Copilot could genuinely add value to the work.

  • Governance and adoption review

    A practical check on whether the ownership and guardrails are in place for people to use Microsoft 365 and AI with confidence.

  • Leadership workshop

    A facilitated session that helps leaders agree on what matters most and where a sensible first step lies.

  • Focused pilot

    A contained test of a new way of working, designed to produce credible evidence before anything is extended further.
